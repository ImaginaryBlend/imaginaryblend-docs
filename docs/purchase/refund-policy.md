# Refund Policy

This refund policy ensures fairness for all customers and creators on Fab. Refunds are typically granted for faulty products or those that do not function as intended.

The [Fab Refund Policy](https://www.fab.com/refund-policy) applies in most cases.

Due to the rising number of fraudulent attempts to demand refunds immediately after purchase for no particular reason, I find it necessary to implement a refund policy to clearly outline the guidelines.

## Refunds are applicable under the following conditions

- A bug in the product prevents you from using it as advertised.
- The description, screenshots, or video on the Fab page do not accurately reflect the item’s content.

## Product limitations

By making a purchase, you acknowledge the product limitations, especially the following:

- The product’s documentation outlines its constraints, and it is essential to review them before purchasing. See [Limitations](/product/limitations).
- Product demos showcase typical use cases for which they were created and thoroughly tested.
- If the product’s capabilities are uncertain, ask questions and ensure it meets your expectations.
- Make sure that the product supports the Unreal Engine version that you use.
- The purchase pertains to the current version of the product, not future planned changes. If a required feature is missing from the current product version, consider holding off on the purchase.
- The creator sells products on Fab ‘as is’ and does not guarantee future updates.
- The creator of the product does not undertake the development of your project.

## The refund will not be approved in the following cases

- *“I thought it would work differently.”*
  Read the documentation and listed limitations before purchasing. In case of ambiguity, inquire on the product page to ensure your expectations can be met.
- *“I wanted it to be more realistic.”*
  Before purchasing, test the executable demo, which shows the product’s capabilities. The demo is available on [itch.io](https://imaginaryblend.itch.io/fluid-flux).
- *“I didn’t know it wouldn’t work on mobile, VR, path tracing, ray tracing, Lumen or Sequencer.”*
  Customers must review the description and ask about specific technologies or design details before purchasing. See [Compatibility](/product/limitations#compatibility).
- *“I’m a beginner; I don’t know how to use the engine; nothing works; I need tutorials.”*
  The product is intended for experienced Unreal Engine users. Support questions will be answered, but the creator is not obligated to teach how the engine works.
- *“I wanted to create an open-world multiplayer, but it’s impossible.”*
  Creating open-world games is challenging due to the extensive design and development required to craft a seamless and immersive environment. The requirements cannot be precisely defined. By deciding on this genre, you implicitly accept the difficulties associated with the problems you will encounter.
- *“The product was expensive, and I need my money back now.”*
  Please do not act impulsively – make an informed decision before purchasing the product.
- *“I did not read the refund policy before the purchase.”*
  Not being aware of the refund policy is not a valid reason for a refund.

## Refund process

- Only Epic can issue refunds for Fab products.
- Before requesting a refund, contact support at [imaginaryblend@gmail.com](mailto:imaginaryblend@gmail.com) or use the *Questions* section on the Fab product page.
- Refund requests must be submitted as a [Refund Request Case](https://support.fab.com/s/?ProductOrigin=FabSupport) within 14 days of the transaction date.
- The seller has the right to request verification of your refund history to determine whether it is fraudulent.
- When the marketplace support team leaves the refund decision to the seller, the *Imaginary Blend Refund Policy* applies.

## In case of issues with downloading the product

- Use the Fab plugin built into Unreal Engine to download the product.
- Ensure the product supports the engine version you are trying to use.
- Restart the Epic Games Launcher to refresh the list of products.
- Contact the marketplace support team to resolve the issue.
