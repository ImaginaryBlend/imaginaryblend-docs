# Bug report form

Fill in the form to prepare a complete bug report. **Prepare email** opens your mail program with the report addressed to support, **Open in Gmail** does the same in Gmail in your browser, and **Copy report** copies it so you can paste it anywhere, for example into your Discord thread. Nothing is sent from this page.

Not sure what to write? [Reporting a bug](/help/reporting-bugs) explains what a good report contains and how to prepare a minimal project.
