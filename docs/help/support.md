# Support

Need help with Fluid Flux? This page explains how to contact me and how to get an answer quickly. Support is available for customers who bought Fluid Flux on Fab – if you have any issues, don’t hesitate to contact me.

Many problems already have a known solution, so check the [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) page first.

The **support email** is the preferred way to contact me. Please don’t ask for support in the comment sections on YouTube or X.

::: info Response time and verification
I always answer support questions, but my availability is limited due to the high volume of requests, so an answer may take a few days. If you don’t hear back, send your message again – it might have gone to spam or I might have missed it.

Support is available only for customers using the product from legal sources. I may ask for your **order ID** to verify the purchase.
:::

## Getting a faster answer

1. **Be precise** – give me as much information as possible. [Reporting a bug](/help/reporting-bugs) lists what to include.
2. **Focus on a single problem** – don’t stack multiple questions at once; let’s solve them one by one.
3. **Be polite and patient** – clear and friendly requests are always answered faster.

## Discord support threads

The Discord support channel is based on **thread conversations** – every question starts in its own thread, which makes it easier to follow separate discussions.

1. Use the support channel **only if you have bought the product** and need help. Use the **general** channel to talk with other people.
2. After you send a question to the support channel, the bot creates a new thread for it.
3. Add the details from [Reporting a bug](/help/reporting-bugs#what-to-include) in your thread.
4. Write only in your thread and don’t start another one until your problem is solved.
